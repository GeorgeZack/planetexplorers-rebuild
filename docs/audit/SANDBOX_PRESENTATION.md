# Sandbox presentation and host boundaries

Sandbox and Tutorial share recovered presentation, not gameplay state. [Feature contracts](../FEATURE_CONTRACTS.md) owns supported behavior, [PROJECT_MAP](../../PROJECT_MAP.md) module ownership, and [PLAYTEST](../PLAYTEST.md) observed acceptance. Early tables describing absent HP, categories, quickslots, actors or Adventure combat are obsolete.

## Preserve source mode distinctions

Adventure, Single Player Build, Tutorial and Adventure's B construction mode are distinct. Keep Tutorial grants, authored targets, completion flags and cleanup mode-owned. Archived Adventure appearances/placements are one world's realized data, not procedural NPC generation. Native pinned hunt arbitration remains an adapter.

## Reuse assets through identity and clock contracts

Require exact source item/prototype/instance, sex, attachment/mount transform and complete named clip clocks. Body, held/stowed equipment and incoming defence share authoritative pose/yaw; tool/melee clocks override locomotion. Missing optional groups keep their documented adapter; malformed present groups fail closed. Never copy an old isolated scene over a newer complete group.

[Actors](ACTOR_RECOVERY.md), [player assets](PLAYER_ASSET_RECOVERY.md), [locomotion](PLAYER_STRAFING.md), [materials](ACTOR_MATERIALS.md), [tool presentation](SANDBOX_TOOL_PRESENTATION.md) and [complete starter-pair staging](STARTER_SWORD_POSE_GROUP.md) own details. Native whole-pose blending does not establish Unity masked humanoid composition or shield readiness.

## Shared presentation state and input

Shared UI owners consume read-only mode snapshots and use one layout for rendering/hit testing. Hosts own progression and transactions. Modal press ownership survives closing through every held/release frame; no UI gesture may leak into tool, attack, dig or placement. Nonmodal consumables suppress actions without pausing movement/AI. See [shared UI](SHARED_UI.md).

## Hostile AI integration

[Bounded hunt](ADVENTURE_HUNT_COMBAT.md) integrates source sensors, guide clocks, post-movement capsule history, authoritative HP and real death credit. Its pinned cohort, scheduling, threat and simultaneous-attack limits are explicit adapters. General NPC combat, pounce/aggression, physical reactions and Town Defense remain closed foundations. Source stats, observed appearance and staged clips alone do not authorize them.

## Art portability and rendering cost

Export offline with exact source hashes/IDs. Preserve coordinate/winding, materials/alpha, skeleton/skin bindings and attachment clocks. Renderer/GPU state stays outside saved simulation; resource loading fails atomically. Recover only required bundles/groups rather than loading the entire installation. Asset extraction and numerical validation precede appropriate visual comparison; neither establishes full shaders/lighting/animation parity.

## Acceptance

Use each feature's contract and evidence. [HANDOFF](../HANDOFF.md) is the single next-work owner. Do not revive early reuse plans or blanket missing-feature claims from this audit's original checkpoint.
