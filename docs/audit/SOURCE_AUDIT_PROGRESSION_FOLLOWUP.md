## 1. Vendor wallets are recoverable now, including their refill lifecycle

**Implemented C:** [pe_town_shop_buy](../../rebuild/src/pe_town_interaction.c#L42) stages player inventory, debits player currency and reduces stock. There is no NPC wallet, sale or buyback owner. This is already a source difference for ordinary purchases: [UIShopWnd.Buy](../../Assets/Scripts/Chenzhi/NewUI/Scripts/PE_GameUI/NGUIScripts/UIShopWnd.cs#L574) credits the NPC purse after successful purchase, not only after buyback.

**Fresh source findings:** [PackageCmpt.Serialize](../../Assets/Scripts/PeEntity/Cmpt/PackageCmpt.cs#L77) writes a length-prefixed Money blob; [Money.Export](../../Assets/PeWorld/PeMoney.cs#L79) stores its signed32-bit digital balance. [NpcPackageCmpt.Serialize](../../Assets/Scripts/PeEntity/Cmpt/NpcPackageCmpt.cs#L375) then writes public slots, private slots, optional automatic-increase data and hand-in slots. The automatic record is two integers (`mMax`, `mIncPerDay`) and one Boolean (`mAdded`). These fields survive reload through the matching component deserializer; no new RNG draw is necessary to recreate an archived resident's purse.

[AutoIncreaseMoney.Update](../../Assets/Scripts/PeEntity/Cmpt/NpcPackageCmpt.cs#L28) clears `mAdded` in the first half of the configured game day, then adds the selected daily amount once in the second half. This is an observed-update latch, not elapsed-day catch-up or a shop-open timer. Initialization sets `mAdded=true` ([line87](../../Assets/Scripts/PeEntity/Cmpt/NpcPackageCmpt.cs#L87)); saved residents must retain their saved value. **Despite its name, `mMax` is never applied by this update.** The digital balance setter also assigns without a maximum ([PeMoney.cs:49](../../Assets/PeWorld/PeMoney.cs#L49)). A cap at the serialized maximum would be an intentional native divergence, not recovered behavior. The existing offer refresh timer is a separate owner.

Do not assume every bounded camp repair feature requires implementing staffed player colonies first.

Export supported item material/condition contracts and the specific camp machine's binding/time data. The [active UI](../../Assets/Scripts/Chenzhi/NewUI/Scripts/PE_GameUI/NGUIScripts/UIRepairWnd.cs#L424) validates all material counts, debits them, starts the counter and retains the item. It uses per-material ceiling directly; the [generic Repair helper](../../Assets/Scripts/Items/ItemObj.cs#L1280) additionally has a small-factor epsilon early return. Test the chosen active branch at tiny damage rather than silently merging the formulas. [Completion](../../Assets/Scripts/WuYiqiu/Colony%20System/Scripts/Entities/CSRepair.cs#L191) repairs the same instance, and [Update](../../Assets/Scripts/WuYiqiu/Colony%20System/Scripts/Entities/CSRepair.cs#L369) persists item ID/current/final counter while pausing an inactive machine.

This supports a quote kernel now and an exact-instance timed camp job next. Required follow-up is offline qualification of a real machine's placement, prefab and persisted object/job binding in the available town archives—not an invented inventory-wide instant repair command. Test reservation aliases, material rollback, save/reload of the same instance and remaining time, cancellation policy and one completion. Broader worker bonuses remain separate. A short native interaction check comes after implementation; no original gameplay is required merely to recover material arithmetic.
