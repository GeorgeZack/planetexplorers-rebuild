## 1. Low-health recovery changes the value of retreating

**Source-derived:** `initProp.id=1` grants buff30200046 in `InitBuffList`. Its `skBuff` row has interval1, lifetime−1 and the literal condition `InCoolingForGettingHurt(4) && HP/HPMax < .33`, then adds `HPMax*.01`. At the starter500 maximum this is5 HP per admitted tick. The expression tests the threshold before adding; it does not cap the result to exactly33%. [SkExpFuncSet.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/SkillSystem/SkExpFuncSet.cs), lines110–112, uses `Time.time >= lastHurt+4`; both successful damage functions update the last-hurt timestamp, lines38–40 and90–92. [PESkEntity.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/PeEntity/Cmpt/PESkEntity.cs), lines258–260 and272–285, mounts/unmounts initial buffs; death disables them at448–452. Loading player data restores the player's initial buff list in [SkAliveEntity.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/SkillSystem/SkAliveEntity.cs), lines360–364.

**Implemented C:** [pe_game.c](../../rebuild/src/pe_game.c), lines376–390, advances only the explicit juice and medpack healing/cooldown paths. [pe_game.h](../../rebuild/src/pe_game.h), lines74–75, retains stamina clocks but no last-hurt/auto-recovery buff state. The hunt resolver changes HP without a recovery timestamp. This is a concrete survival omission: a wounded native player cannot recover by retreating under this source rule. It does not explain every recorded death, and recovery would not run while incoming damage repeatedly resets its condition.

**Minimum implementation/test:** export the init/buff contract rather than copying five into host code; add one authoritative scaled gameplay clock and last-hurt timestamp, apply the source scheduler, unmount on death, and specify persistence/migration. Test exactly4s, immediately before4s, just below/equal.33, threshold overshoot, max-HP variation, zero/calculated damage notification semantics, death and save/load. Do not use `game_seconds`, which intentionally advances world time12× in `pe_game.c:378`, as if it were `Time.time`. No engine capture is required for this arithmetic/state slice.

## 2. Stamina recovery is a buff tick, not continuous integration

**Source-derived:** `initProp1` has stamina100, recovery40, reduction multiplier1 and base delay0. Buff30200052 has interval1 and condition `InCoolingForConsumingStamina(StaminaRecoverDelay)`; each admitted evaluation adds `StaminaRecoverSpeed*StaminaReducePercent`. [SkExpFuncSet.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/SkillSystem/SkExpFuncSet.cs), lines114–117, requires strictly greater than the last consumption time plus `max(delay,.1)`. [SkBuffs.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/SkillSystem/SkBuffs.cs), lines159–183, executes the initial evaluation, then at most one overdue interval per global eight-rendered-frame attribute pass with strict `elapsed > n*interval`; it evaluates before expiration. Buff phase therefore matters independently of time since the last attack. See the [lifecycle audit](PASSIVE_RECOVERY.md) for first-execution, mount/load and scheduler details.

Buff30200175 adds `(1-Comfort/ComfortMax)*2` to stamina recovery delay. Comfort begins500/500; buff30200049 changes it every5s by `ComfortRecoverSpeed*ComfortReducePercent*5` (starter speed−.1388). Same-type, equal-priority buffs are not automatically mutually exclusive: the actual mount logic in `SkBuffs.cs:119–134` only rejects higher priority, removes lower priority, or replaces the same ID at its stack limit. Thus30200052 and30200175 need not displace each other. [Action_Sword.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/PeEntity/Cmpt/Motion/PEAction/Action_Sword.cs), lines498–518, refreshes last-consumption time throughout the action, not only on the five-point charge.

**Implemented C:** `pe_game.c:450–459` continuously integrates recovery after its delay; `pe_sandbox_view.c:824` correctly marks active melee as a stamina action. Comfort and its delay modifier are absent. Continuous recovery is not the source's stepwise clock, even though the average40/s matches. Do not infer that one is always harder: phase determines when the first chunk becomes available.

**Minimum implementation/test:** share a bounded buff scheduler with finding1; retain attack/jump/sprint consumption ownership, capture buff phase separately from last-use time, and test strict boundaries, a delayed update, exact one-overdue-tick behavior, changing comfort and simultaneous equal-priority buffs. A small exact comfort/delay subset is possible before general survival, but do not claim full hunger/oxygen/nutrition parity. Those additional initial buffs are independently recoverable database formulas. No new gameplay or pose capture is needed.

## 3. Capsule history is a concrete geometric gap

**Source-derived:** [PECapsuleTrigger.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/Attack/PECapsuleTrigger.cs), lines83–100, retains previous/current endpoints and endpoint motion. Its capsule-against-capsule method, lines146–180, samples `max(floor(.5*endpoint_motion/scaledRadius)+1)` positions. It interpolates endpoint radii about the owner's center when nonzero and tests against the other capsule's current endpoints. For a single sample it uses the previous attacking endpoints (`p=0`), an unusual detail that must not be silently replaced with a conventional continuous sweep. The narrowphase remains strict distance-squared `< radiusSum²` at lines108–119.

[PEAttackTrigger.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/Attack/PEAttackTrigger.cs), lines56–89, updates part information on activation and owns two trigger-history lists; `ClearHitInfo` and `ResetHitInfo` differ. Lines109–119 update parts and contacts in LateUpdate. Skill-level deduplication is separate: [SkInst.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/SkillSystem/SkInst.cs), lines297–313, records an entity only when a collision event successfully executes, once per guide.

**Implemented C:** [pe_hitbox.h](../../rebuild/src/pe_hitbox.h), line37, explicitly declares no swept history. [pe_sandbox_melee.c](../../rebuild/src/pe_sandbox_melee.c), lines140–149, poses the blade at its current clock; [pe_knife_contact.c](../../rebuild/src/pe_knife_contact.c), lines30–49, and [pe_hare_damage.c](../../rebuild/src/pe_hare_damage.c), lines60–83, test current capsules. Entity/guide deduplication already exists and misses do not commit it. First intersecting defence-part selection is also already correct: source collision results set `distance=0`, so `PEAttackTrigger.CheckHit`'s strict comparison retains the first result. Do not change this to nearest/deepest contact without different source authority.

**Minimum implementation/test:** a pure history sampler with explicit update/reset/activation rules, then one shared blade/forearm adapter. Preserve post-movement authoritative poses, deterministic bounds and atomic rollback; avoid a second animation clock or sweeping across loads, teleports or replaced instances. Tests should include intermediate-only hits, count1 previous-endpoint behavior, radius threshold sample counts, rotating endpoints about owner center, zero center, source tangency rejection, trigger reset versus clear, repeated guide suppression and the next guide. Existing baked poses suffice; exact Unity callback phase alignment remains a documented adapter until separately qualified. This can affect both outgoing and incoming hits, so a favorable balance direction is not established.

## Checks against speculative balance changes

- `initProp1.CritPercent=0` and `PrototypeMonster13.CritPercent=0`; source damage does call `RandIn(CritPercent)`, but current noncritical baseline is not automatically a bug. General modified critical stats need an explicit deterministic RNG contract later, not invented starter criticals.
- `PrototypeItem33.durDec=0`, durability100. Knife's lack of per-hit wear is source-correct for that item; Sword1's five-point wear must not be copied to it.
- Source Sword attack admission has no five-stamina minimum in `Action_Sword.cs:87–94`; current `pe_combat.c:75–87` accepts low/zero stamina and clamps its charge. Preserve this while changing recovery cadence.
- `initProp1.SprintSpeed=10` alone does **not** establish that the native8m/s constant is wrong. [Motion_Move_Human.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/PeEntity/Cmpt/Motion/Motion_Move_Human.cs), lines339–360, takes sprint speed from the active style's serialized `MoveParam.m_MoveSpeedList`, otherwise falling back to its default8 at line35. The direct attribute lookup belongs to the separate [Motion_Move_Motor.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/PeEntity/Cmpt/Motion/Motion_Move_Motor.cs), line699. Verify the installed player component and current style entry before changing a speed; exact installed style speed was not newly decoded in this audit.
- Current fixed full-HP six-hare reload/preserved quest count, limited AI arbitration and modal combat pause remain explicit adapters in [ADVENTURE_HUNT_COMBAT](ADVENTURE_HUNT_COMBAT.md). This audit does not turn them into proven source equivalence or explain all difficulty through one missing subsystem.

The next offline implementation order is the shared buff clock/health recovery, source stamina cadence, historical capsule query, then block admission/host preparation and exact contact-speed curves. Export force/reaction data alongside those changes. Use focused numerical, transition, identity and save tests before one final integrated visual check.
