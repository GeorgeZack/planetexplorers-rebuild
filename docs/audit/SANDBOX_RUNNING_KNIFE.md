| [pe_sandbox_actor](../../rebuild/src/pe_sandbox_actor.h) and [sandbox host](../../rebuild/src/pe_sandbox_view.c) | Body/held weapon and incoming defence read the authoritative post-movement pose, clip time and body yaw. Active actions override the locomotion blend. |

A terminal outgoing interval is applied once and is not recomputed from a reset action clock.
